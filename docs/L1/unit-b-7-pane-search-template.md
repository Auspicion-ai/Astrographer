## 1. The unit, and the five `READ` findings that decide its shape

## 2. The two sources, NAMED, and what each may reach

- **`search` — its OWN answer, and no other source.** The producer is `bridge.rag.query` reached through the two submit seams (`submitQuery`, `submitAdvancedQuery` — the app's own `window.provident.sidebar` surface, `READ` `installSidebarBridge`), which COMMIT `lastQueryResult`/`advancedSearchError` and then **FIRE** `search`; `searchAdvancedToggle` fires too (the disclosure is the pane's own state). **REFUSED, with the reason: a local store-change source for this pane.** `onRagStoreChanged` cannot be its producer: nothing in the pane's data is store-derived (`F-1`), so a fire there could only be answered by a WHOLE-STORE read (`snapshot`/`docHeads` — refused: the pane does not render them) or by a re-issue of the pane's own query — and a re-issue on a fire is an **UNBOUNDED LOOP**, because the re-issue's own settlement is itself the producer that fires (`query → fire → query → …`). The channel is payload-free by construction (`ASM-B3-2`), so the handler cannot tell a settlement fire from a store fire; the loop is therefore structural, not a discipline.
