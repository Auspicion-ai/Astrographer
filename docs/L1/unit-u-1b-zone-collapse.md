## 1. The unit, and THE LAYER RULING (the opposite of `U-1a`'s)

- **The act, under D-22 (1): ONE collapsed state, TWO ways to reach it.** Three parts: **(1) the statement** — an optional member on the zone record (`collapsed?: boolean`), absent = "nothing stated", present = the operator's word, written by the act alone and **never deleted** (`forking.md` §7.1, lines 476-479); **(2) the derivation** — where nothing is stated, a zone is collapsed **iff no enabled+placed pane names it**, read off the census the `is-empty` mirror already uses; **(3) the control** — the anchored hide arrow as a **SIBLING** of its zone container in the authored envelope, positioned against its own zone and overlaying its foot, with **no resize strip on a collapsed zone** (lines 495-498; change-analysis §2.1, §2.2, §2.6). **The fork's `minimized` does not survive beside the member:** "minimize an occupied zone to a tab strip" is the act's *overturning* of the derivation on the same member, and the tab-strip paint is the collapsed-zone paint (D-22 (1)).
- **The remembered `size` is never touched** (lines 480-481): collapse writes no size, expansion costs zero writes.
- **THE LAYER RULING — `U-1b` runs the FULL nested loop: L1's contract, then L2 (`docs/L2/shell-chrome/`), then L3 (`docs/L3/shell-chrome/`), then L4, one module (`shell-chrome`), no sibling read (D-3, D-4, D-7).** Three reasons: **(a)** it authors a **behavioural I/O surface** — an act with an address and a write discipline, a derivation with a named input, and a paint with two axes — which is what L2 designs and `U-1a`'s rename does not have; **(b)** its state member changes **shape** (a required total boolean becomes an optional statement), so a cell of `P-SM-2` must be re-derived by a layer that owns the module's state model; **(c)** its evidence is **split across classes** — node arms for the derivation, the act and the authored node, but a **G8 row** for the paint — and only an L3 pass can state which guardrail note belongs to which (`RCA-12`).
- **Rejected alternative:** folding `U-1b` into `U-1a` as one L1-only unit, which is the fused `U-1` this re-shape exists to undo (change-analysis §3, RCA-2/RCA-5).

### `C-4` the collapsed EXTENT — **written in the ONE-state arm (D-22 (1))**

- **`auto` on the axis the zone fixes, `hidden` on its arrangement axis** (`forking.md` lines 482-484), expressed in the fork's own projection: the collapsed zone's **track token reads `auto`** (`--zone-<zone>-track: auto`), so the grid area and the `position: fixed` container box (whose insets are the same tokens) both take their extent from content; the arrangement axis's overflow goes `hidden` (`overflow-y` for the side zones, `overflow-x` for the top/bottom zones, by the authored `data-orientation`).
- **THE TOKEN IS AUTHORED TWICE, AND IT MUST BE, or the extent never paints (`ASM-U1b-10`, ruled after `L2`'s finding F1).** The fork writes `--zone-<z>-track` **inline** on the grid element at every `setLayout` (`sidebar-panes.ts`'s `applyZoneTracks` composing `zoneTrackVars`), and an inline custom property outranks any stylesheet rule — so a stylesheet-only `auto` would be authoritative before the first write and never after. Therefore **(a)** `zoneTrackVars` (`layout-state.ts`) emits `'auto'` for a **collapsed occupied** zone (`counts === 0` still takes F-3's `'0px'`/`''` arms unchanged, and an expanded occupied zone still takes `size + 'px'`), reading the state through the **same** `zoneCollapsedOf` predicate; and **(b)** `index.html` carries the declarative `:has(...)` mirror of the same arms, which is the pre-first-write and re-mount path — exactly the dual authoring F-3 already landed. Two arms, **one predicate**, one vocabulary. **The projection's `size`-write half is a source reading, not a limb** (`L3`'s `N4`): the `auto` branch writes no `size`, and limb `H` grades the four values, not the absence of that write.
- **No `DEFAULT_ZONE_COLLAPSED_SIZE`-shaped constant and no `size` write, ever** (`ASM-U1b-4`, `G`).
- **THE RECONCILIATION WITH F-3, stated because the two mechanisms author one extent (`ASM-U1b-8`, mechanism corrected in the `G5` repair pass):** the **empty** zone's extent stays the landed `0px`-track derivation (`:has([zone='x'].is-empty:not(.is-revealed))` → `0px`, carried by a live `PASS`). **The mechanism is a GATE, not an order:** the two rules **tie** at measured specificity `(2,3,0)`, so declaration order decides nothing — the arms are made mutually exclusive by construction, the `auto` rule requiring **`:not(.is-empty)`** and F-3's requiring `.is-empty`, which leaves the third state (`is-empty` **+** `is-revealed`) with no extent rule at all and the base `var(--zone-<x>-size, …)` fallback supplying the persisted size. That fallback is exactly the arm `zoneTrackVars`' `''` removal path hands it, so the two authors agree in all four arms: empty+revealed ⇒ persisted size; empty+not-revealed ⇒ `0px` (F-3, the authority for that case); collapsed occupied ⇒ `auto` (its tab strip's own extent); expanded occupied ⇒ persisted size. `ASM-U1b-4` forbids porting `auto` *over* the empty case, and the first landing of this clause did exactly that in the revealed arm — a childless `auto` box of zero width, i.e. an unhittable drop target (the `G5` `BLOCKING` finding, fixed before the unit's report). **The ruled asymmetry (`L2` finding F2, closed here):** an **empty** zone stated `false` is expanded in the record **and** in the envelope (`is-collapsed` absent), yet its extent is still F-3's `0px` — the statement becomes visible the moment the zone gains a pane. That is the ruling, not a contradiction: the empty-track policy is an *emptiness* fact (a defect fix with a live `PASS`), never a second statement of the collapse fact.
- **Out of this unit, named so no later unit invents it:** the preview band's remembered-`size` composition and the drop-into-a-collapsed-zone zone-first chain (lines 485-491) belong to `U-3`, which consumes this unit's write discipline.
