## 2. The two sources, the discriminator, and what each arm may reach

**The branches, in this ORDER, and the order is the claim** (spelled once, at `onRagStoreChanged`'s head):

1. **THE ENGINE BRANCH — exact equality, fail-closed, FIRST.** `payload.store === <the engine-origin label>` → `this.events?.publishChange()` → **`return`**. It reaches ONLY the change-interested panes (`changePanes()`), through `fire`, so it cannot reach a pane that did not declare change interest. **It does NOT accumulate `pendingContentChange` and does NOT `requestRebuild`.** The refusal is load-bearing: an engine descriptor's `nodeIds` are the ENGINE's document ids and its `kind` is the mapping's (`READ` `src/main/main.ts`'s reconcile answer: `kind:'structural'`, `nodeIds` = the engine CRUD walk, `edgeIds: []`), so feeding it to the local re-derive would re-assemble the app graph around ids no local store carries — the wrong-origin harm `B-4`'s `B4-EX-2` pinned fail-closed on the main side, refused here on the renderer side.
2. **The three local guards, UNCHANGED** (F-1): `lastStore === null` → warn+return · a malformed `store` → warn+return · `payload.store !== this.lastStore` → silent return. **Every non-engine foreign name stays dropped** — that is the anti-masquerade fence's second direction, and it is why `'gnosis-engine'` (registry-legal, one character from the label) must stay a LOCAL name.
3. **`pendingContentChange` commit → `fire('doc-nav')` → `requestRebuild('content')`**, in that order and for `B-5` §4's reason (the fire's IPC is ISSUED before the re-derive's assemble, so any later assemble authors the pane from fresh state). The fire is **ADDITIVE** (`ASM-B5-2`'s twin, `ASM-B6-2`): the rebuild stays, because a structural write moves things this unit does not own.

**The discriminator is the `store` member and nothing else.** No `kind`, no `nodeIds`, no `edgeIds` decides which arm runs; a frame from one source cannot masquerade as the other because the two arms are reached by two DIFFERENT entries (`fire` vs `publishChange`) on ONE registry, and `B-4` pinned the label so the equality cannot be satisfied by an impostor (`'gnosis:engine2'`, `'gnosis-engine'`, `''`, a non-string, a colon-free registry name). **`ASM-B6-1` states the label's home in the renderer** (§5, and **`S2`** grades it).

## 3. The re-read: ONE call, a GENERATION STAMP, and the two failure disciplines

- **The re-read:** `paintReplumbedPane('doc-nav')` gains ONE arm beside `crosslinks`' — `const answer = await this.bridge.rag.docHeads()` → **on a stamp match** commit `this.lastDocHeads = answer.documents` → then `updatePane('doc-nav')`. **No snapshot, no security, no traversal, no template, no backlinks, no `decorateShared`, no `syncZoneMirrors`.** A bridge error keeps the last-known list (the `refresh()` discipline, one method away) and the paint still runs (it may lawfully answer `unchanged`); a non-object or a `documents`-less answer is committed as `[]` only if it is an ARRAY, else the last-known list is kept — the pane authors `(no documents)` from `null`, never a throw (`READ` `docNavContent`'s H1/`LOW-2` arms).
- **`ASM-B6-3` — THE GENERATION STAMP, and it is this unit's ONE structure.** An UNCOALESCED fire (F-4's cost) puts N `docHeads` answers in flight; they can land out of order and a stale one committed LAST paints a stale list **with no later fire to correct it** — the reachable twin of `ASM-B5-4(i)` and the reason B-5's subject stamp does not transfer (this pane has no node subject; its subject is the list itself). The fix: a host-owned monotone counter, captured before the `await` and compared after; **the answer that no longer holds the newest generation is DROPPED — not committed, not painted** — and the newest always commits and always paints. Two call sites, one rule, no timer and no queue (`D-82` clause 4).
- **The re-read's GATE is the paint's own census, and that is a decision:** the arm runs only when the registry knows the id, its scope is `app-graph`, and it is ENABLED — the three cheap facts `paintPane` answers `not-a-pane`/`operator-scope`/`not-enabled` with. A disabled pane renders no frame, so a re-read for it is an IPC that can change nothing (`D9`). The words `not-mounted`/`no-runtime` are NOT gated on: they are transient boot states, and gating on them would restate `paintPane`'s steps 4–5 one level up.
- **Named limit, not defended:** `bridge.rag.docHeads` that never settles still starves this pane's paint — `invoke`-hung bridges are the bridge's defect and no timer is admissible (`D-82` clause 4; `ASM-B5-4(c)`'s shape).
