## 1. The unit, the split, and the layer ruling

- **The act:** the D-16 zone rename, **whole**, over the fork's own tree — `LayoutZoneName` becomes `'top' | 'bottom' | 'left' | 'right'`, the carrier becomes `[zone=]`, the MCP-visible ids become `zone:top`/`zone:bottom`, the track/size tokens and the grid areas follow (D-14, D-16). **The persisted-slice migration the rename would force is NOT part of it: the user REFUSED it (D-22 (2)) and ACCEPTED the silent relocation, recorded as `docs/defects.md`'s `ZONE-RENAME-SILENTLY-RELOCATES-PERSISTED-PLACEMENTS`.** `LAYOUT_VERSION` stays **1** and no migration arm is written.
- **Why it is split off from the behaviour:** a ~300-site rename plus a behaviour change in one commit is the pattern RCA-2/RCA-5 exist to prevent; the fused unit could not claim a red-before-green for either half (change-analysis §3). **What moves with the name:** `panes[].order` → `panes[].index`, a **spelling** move over a settled meaning (`layout-state.ts:46-53`), and — because D-22 (1) settles the collapse MEANING on the foundation's `collapsed` — that member's renaming belongs to `U-1b`, not here.
- **What does NOT move, stated so no later sweep invents it:** `minimized`, `data-minimized`, `is-minimized`, `zoneMinimizeToggle`/`paneTabExpand`, the ONE-state collapse and the `auto`/`hidden` extent — `U-1b`'s (D-22 (1), §3 of the change-analysis) — and the theme carrier, `U-1c`'s.
- **THE LAYER RULING — `U-1a` runs at L1 with NO L2 and NO L3 pass, and this is the ruling, not a skipped step.** Three falsifiable reasons: **(a)** it authors **no new I/O surface** — every mapping is total and statically determined by the type and the enumeration, and under D-22 it adds **no** behaviour at all (the migration that would have been its one branch is refused); **(b)** an L3 pass would author a **second authority** over a mechanical mapping (the same reasoning `unit-v-1-pin-restate.md` §1 records for a value move) — the design is the change set in §2, and a pseudocode restatement of it is the duplication `unit-foundation-rerevendor-2026-10-04.md` §4 forbids; **(c)** the evidence class cannot be lifted: whatever an L2/L3 pass wrote, this unit's ceiling stays envelope/DOC and it can never be app-green (`RCA-12`, D-10). **`U-1b` is the opposite case and says so in its own §1** — it carries behaviour and runs the full L2→L3→L4 chain.
- **Rejected alternative:** running `U-1a` under `U-1b`'s L2 to design "the zone vocabulary change" as an integration surface. Rejected because the surface does not change: the same six seams (`[zone=]`, `zone:<name>`, the track family, the census, the geometry readers, the driver) keep their shapes and change only their values — a rename that changed a surface shape would be a different unit and is not admitted here.
