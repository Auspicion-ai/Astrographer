## 1. The unit, the split, and the layer ruling

- **The act:** the D-16 zone rename, **whole**, over the fork's own tree — `LayoutZoneName` becomes `'top' | 'bottom' | 'left' | 'right'`, the carrier becomes `[zone=]`, the MCP-visible ids become `zone:top`/`zone:bottom`, the track/size tokens and the grid areas follow (D-14, D-16) — **plus the persisted-slice migration the rename forces**, because without it every existing install silently relocates its `header`/`footer` panes into `left` on first load (change-analysis §2.3; `layout-state.ts:200`, `:206-212`).
- **Why it is split off from the behaviour:** a ~300-site rename plus a behaviour change in one commit is the pattern RCA-2/RCA-5 exist to prevent; the fused unit could not claim a red-before-green for either half (change-analysis §3). **What moves with the name:** `panes[].order` → `panes[].index`, a **spelling** move over a settled meaning (`layout-state.ts:46-53`).
- **What does NOT move, stated so no later sweep invents it:** `minimized`, `data-minimized`, `is-minimized`, `zoneMinimizeToggle`/`paneTabExpand` — those are `U-1b`'s, because the word `collapsed` has an unsettled MEANING (D-16's own escalate-don't-rename clause; change-analysis §2.2/§2.9) — and the theme carrier, which is `U-1c`'s.
- **THE LAYER RULING — `U-1a` runs at L1 with NO L2 and NO L3 pass, and this is the ruling, not a skipped step.** Three falsifiable reasons: **(a)** it authors **no new I/O surface** — every mapping is total, statically determined by the type and the enumeration, and the one behaviour it adds (the v1→v2 migration) is a branch of an existing total function whose contract `P-SM-2` already pins; **(b)** an L3 pass would author a **second authority** over a mechanical mapping (the same reasoning `unit-v-1-pin-restate.md` §1 records for a value move) — the design is the change set in §2, and a pseudocode restatement of it is the duplication `unit-foundation-rerevendor-2026-10-04.md` §4 forbids; **(c)** the evidence class cannot be lifted: whatever an L2/L3 pass wrote, this unit's ceiling stays envelope/DOC and it can never be app-green (`RCA-12`, D-10). **`U-1b` is the opposite case and says so in its own §1** — it carries behaviour and runs the full L2→L3→L4 chain.
- **Rejected alternative:** running `U-1a` under `U-1b`'s L2 to design "the zone vocabulary change" as an integration surface. Rejected because the surface does not change: the same six seams (`[zone=]`, `zone:<name>`, the track family, the census, the geometry readers, the driver) keep their shapes and change only their values — a rename that changed a surface shape would be a different unit and is not admitted here.

## 6. Assumptions, each with the alternative it rejected

- **`ASM-U1a-1`** — the payload enumeration is re-grained **by position** (`['left','right','top','bottom']`, i.e. `header→top`, `footer→bottom` in place), so the assembler's payload order and every order-sensitive assertion keep their meaning. **Rejected:** adopting the foundation's `top|bottom|left|right` order, which reorders the payload, the four `zones` literals and the payload-order rows for no ruling's sake — `DECLARED_ZONE_IDS`' order is the foundation's **demo census**, which `forking.md` §9 says a fork must not copy.
- **`ASM-U1a-2`** — the two axis readers are **narrowed** to `LayoutZoneName` (`layout-zone-geometry.ts:22`, `pane-drag.ts:237`). **Rejected:** leaving them `unknown` (the `pane-gutter.ts:85-98` contrast the critique drew), which makes a missed rename **compile** and misbehave silently in the shim's blind spot.
- **`ASM-U1a-3`** — `[zone=]` **replaces** `data-zone`; no alias, no dual read. **Rejected:** keeping `data-zone` as a compatibility alias, which is the two-vocabulary tree D-14 forbids and would make the census tooth untestable.
- **`ASM-U1a-4`** — the unit's own row lives under `tests/integration/shell-chrome/`. **Rejected:** `tests/e2e/` (L1's declared path but absent, unclaimed, and would be reported by the diff-scope lint — `ASM-L1-4`).
- **`ASM-U1a-5`** — the **grid AREA names** move with the zones (`top`/`bottom`), re-graining greens `E-03`/`E-05`. **Rejected:** keeping the CSS-internal names `header`/`footer` while the carriers move — two vocabularies inside one file, and the exact split D-14 forbids; the cost is that `unit-zone-replacement-greens.md`'s verbatim `PASS` strings are re-stated beside the superseded ones (annotate-beside, `RCA-8(c)`).
- **`ASM-U1a-6`** — the migration sits in `coerceLayout` behind a `LAYOUT_VERSION` bump, **pending question 2**; an unfavourable answer converts §2 row 2 into a recorded acceptance and leaves `LAYOUT_VERSION` at `1`. **Rejected:** migrating in the store, which would move a pinned byte (`operator-settings-store.ts`, md5 row `:138`) and split the record's authority across two modules.
