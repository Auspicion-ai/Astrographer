## 1. The unit

- **The act:** the fork's consumer of the engine's change feed. `GET /changes` is a registered route whose handler sends a **cursor-first frame then one `change` frame per committed journal entry, connection held open** (`READ` `../Gnosis/src/bin/gnosis_server.rs:1056` the route; `:970-1040` the handler; `:983-986` the cursor frame `event: cursor` + `{"type":"cursor","cursor":"<decimal>"}`; `:936-950` `change_frame`'s key set `{type, cursor, wikiId, kind, nodeIds, edgeIds, timestamp}`; `:1005-1032` the poll loop — **no keep-alive, no `done`, no coalescing, 100 ms interval** at `:930`; `:1035-1039` `text/event-stream` exactly). `cursor` is a **decimal STRING** (`:939` `entry.seq.to_string()` + `:984` `Value::String`), `wikiId` is **nullable** (`:940`), `timestamp` is an ISO-8601 string (`READ` `../Gnosis/src/store/mod.rs:1094-1095`), `kind` is the entry's own `op` literal from a **closed 20-token set** (`READ` `../Gnosis/src/store/mod.rs:1085-1091`; the set printed at `../Gnosis/docs/specs/u4-cursor-paged-reads.md:512-517`), and `edgeIds` are the **six-term canonical JSON-array strings** (`:942` `e.encode()`; encoding pinned `u4-cursor-paged-reads.md:542-548`).
- **What the fork lacks and this unit supplies, in four limbs:** (1) a **consumer** that parses those two frame families and maps each `change` frame to the fork's OWN change descriptor; (2) **cursor de-dup**, because a reconnect replays the retained window `(floor, head]` (`READ` `:959-963`, `:992-995`); (3) **reconnect by RE-READ**, because the feed accepts **no request-side cursor** — verbatim `the feed has no request-side cursor today (§9 item 4)` (`READ` `:972-976`) — so a reconnecting subscriber cannot prove it missed nothing (`ASM-B-4`, map §8); (4) the **wiring** to the panes through `B-3`'s channel, never a re-assembly (`INV-E`).
- **The fork's descriptor vocabulary is FIXED and is not this unit's to widen:** `RagStoreChangedPayload = { kind: 'content' | 'structural'; nodeIds: string[]; edgeIds: string[]; store: string }` (`READ` `src/shared/types.ts:415-421`; `store` REQUIRED, `:419-420`), emitted today by `main.ts` (`READ` `:601-607`, `:958-959`, `:996-997`, `:1020-1021`, `:1233-1234`) and by the MCP edit branch (`READ` `src/main/mcp-server.ts:1401-1415`, `:2457`). **`src/shared/types.ts` is out of scope, so the descriptor IS that four-member shape — the mapping must fit it, not extend it.**
- **What this unit does NOT do:** it paints no pane, declares no pane's change interest (that is `B-6`/`B-8`, map §4), writes no record entry, opens no wiki, re-polls nothing, and files no engine request (GR-5/`GRQ-10`/`GRQ-11` already carry the route and its restart semantics — `RECORDED` `docs/feature-requests/gnosis-engine-prerequisites.md:232-262`; the map's `ASM-B-4` refuses a duplicate request in terms).

## 2. The consumer — `src/main/engine-changes.ts` (new; node-testable, no Electron)

- **The surface (names and behaviour are this contract's; signatures are L2's):** `createEngineChangesFeed(opts)` returning a feed handle with **`connect()`** (opens; nothing is applied until the connection's announcement starts the re-read of §2.4 and its answer lands), **`close()`** (total, idempotent), **`status()`** (the head/watermark + the last typed failure, a COPY) and **`census()`** (frames seen / applied / dropped-by-cursor / unknown-kind / connects, a COPY). `opts` carries the baseUrl, an optional `auth.token`, an optional injected `sse: SseClient` (the test seam — the SAME injection `createEngineRagStore` uses, `READ` `src/main/engine-rag-store.ts:285`), a REQUIRED **`reconcile`** (§2.4), and a REQUIRED **`onChange(descriptor)`** sink (§2.2). **Pure function of its `opts` plus the stream: no Electron import, no DOM, no filesystem, no timer.**
- **§2.1 THE TWO FRAME FAMILIES.** A frame arrives from the `SseClient` as `(event, data)` (`READ` `src/main/engine-rag-store.ts:194-203`). `event: cursor` is a **position announcement**: it parses to `{type:'cursor', cursor:'<decimal>'}` and **emits NOTHING** — it records the connection's **head** (the watermark the frames of ITS connection are judged against, §2.3) and **STARTS the re-read** (§2.4) and is counted. `event: change` parses to the exact seven-key body and is mapped. ⟨**AMENDED BY MEASUREMENT `2026-11-18` (the landing's `F-5`), and the amendment is the whole of §2.4's anchor:** the announcement is read by the engine at `store.change_cursor()` **before** the window `(floor, head]` is framed into the SAME body (`READ` `../Gnosis/src/bin/gnosis_server.rs:977-996`), so its value is the head at THAT instant — which is why the re-read must be anchored to it and not to the caller's `connect()`.**⟩ Anything else (`event: result|done|error`, a label/data-type mismatch, malformed JSON, a missing/extra key) is a **typed frame refusal** — never a fabricated descriptor, never a stream teardown (`u4-cursor-paged-reads.md:521-524` requires the consumer to tolerate what it does not know; `parseSseFrame` already enforces the two-line shape, `READ` `engine-rag-store.ts:705-732`).
- **§2.2 THE MAPPING, and it is this unit's decision.** Each `change` frame yields the fork's descriptor with **exactly four keys** — mirroring the fork's own exactness discipline:
  - `nodeIds`/`edgeIds` are **carried verbatim** (both are string arrays on both sides; `edgeIds` are the engine's six-term JSON strings, which the fork's `string[]` holds unchanged — no re-encoding, no parsing).
  - `kind` is the **closed table** below. `content` is claimed **only** where the entry rewrites node TEXT and moves no graph shape: `update_document`, `update_fact`, `update_community_summary`, `re_sync_embed`. The other sixteen appended tokens (`create_document`, `delete_document`, `publish_document`, `unpublish_document`, `archive_document`, `create_wiki`, `add_triple`, `declare_community`, `set_reference_state`, `resolve_entities`, `merge_facts`, `create_fact`, `propose_candidate_fact`, `re_derive_community`, `propagate_fact_staleness`, `propagate_archived_target`) map to **`structural`**. **An UNKNOWN token maps to `structural` — never dropped, never thrown, and counted** (`u4-cursor-paged-reads.md:521-524`): mislabelling a structural change as `content` makes the renderer walk a stale graph, so the asymmetry is deliberate and the wider invalidation is the safe arm (`READ` the fork's consumer of `kind`: `src/renderer/content-reconcile.ts:367-374`, `:562-566`).
  - `store` is the **engine-origin label**, a pinned constant `'gnosis:engine'` — and it is **PROVABLY not a registry name**: `RAG_STORE_NAME_PATTERN = /^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9_-]{0,63}$/` (`READ` `src/main/rag-store-registry.ts:58-60`) refuses a colon. That is what keeps `B-5`'s fail-closed foreign-store guard (`READ` `src/renderer/sidebar-panes.ts:3185-3189`) from ever accepting an engine change as a LOCAL store change — an engine-origin frame must never re-derive the local graph.
  - **`cursor`, `wikiId`, `timestamp`, `type` do NOT cross the wire.** The cursor is the consumer's private position (§2.3); `wikiId` names the engine's wiki, not a fork store; `timestamp` has no reader at this head. A descriptor carrying any of them is a refusal.
- **§2.3 CURSOR DE-DUP.** The consumer holds `lastCursor` — the watermark §2.1's announcement sets for its connection and §2.2's applied frames advance **monotonically**. A `change` frame whose cursor is **at or below** `lastCursor` is **DROPPED and counted** — not re-applied, not re-emitted, not an error — and the drop is **lawful only because the re-read covers everything `≤` the head** (§2.4): a replay of a frame the consumer already applied and a window frame the re-read already answered are one and the same case here, and neither may reach a pane twice. The comparison is **NUMERIC over the decimal token, never lexicographic** (`'10'` is above `'9'`; a string compare would drop every cursor after the first `single-digit < multi-digit` boundary — a row drives exactly that pair). A cursor that is **not** the canonical decimal (`u4-cursor-paged-reads.md:667-671`'s grammar) is a frame refusal, not a silent coercion; a cursor that moves BACKWARDS below `lastCursor` is a drop, and `lastCursor` never moves backwards.
- **§2.4 RECONNECT BY RE-READ (`ASM-B-4`), and it is the invariant that makes a reconnect lawful — ANCHORED TO THE ANNOUNCEMENT, never to `connect()`.** The route accepts no request-side cursor (`READ` `:972-976`), so a subscriber cannot prove it missed nothing and **no frame is ever trusted as a delta claim**. The anchor is load-bearing and this contract's own, amended by measurement at the landing (`F-5`): the engine reads the head (`:977`) and frames the announcement **with** the retained window in one body (`:983-996`), so a re-read that ran BEFORE the subscription is blind to exactly the commits that landed between its read and that head read — those are `≤ head` (⇒ dropped by §2.3) **and** absent from the re-read's answer, i.e. a silent gap of the very kind `ASM-B-4` forbids. **The order is therefore, per connect:** `subscribe` → the announcement records the head **and starts `reconcile()`** → **no `change` frame is applied until the reconcile's answer has been delivered to the sink** → then §2.3 judges frames against that head: **at or below ⇒ dropped *because the re-read covers it*; above ⇒ applied, exactly once.** Every commit is then either `≤ head` (inside a re-read that STARTED after the head was read) or `> head` (framed live by the poll loop, `:1018-1031`) — **no gap and no double-apply.** ⟨**CORRECTED AT THE LANDING (`O-4`, the landing's own finding against this contract):** the ordering limb needs the frames that arrive *while the answer is in flight* to be **HELD** — so the earlier phrase "no queue" was too strong and is replaced by its exact shape: **a ONE-SHOT HOLD, not a queue** — ordered, liveness-gated, discarded on retirement, with **no timer, no retry, no re-arm and no second read** (D-82 clause 4 forbids the timer, not the hold; a hold that can re-arm would be a queue and is refused).**⟩ A connection that never announces applies **nothing** and says so on `status()` (nothing can be framed without it, `:983-996`), and a reconcile that fails is **fail-closed**: the subscription is closed and the typed failure settles `status()`. The callable is INJECTED rather than built here because the full reconcile is the engine CRUD read (wikis, then each wiki's documents — `READ` `src/main/engine-crud-rag-store.ts:772-779`, **paged**: a one-page read is a partial reconcile and a row pins the walk's bound), which belongs to that module and not to this one.
- **§2.5 NO RETRY, NO TIMER, NO SECOND CONNECTION.** `createSseClient` **never auto-reconnects** and its docblock says so (`READ` `src/main/engine-rag-store.ts:758-762`, `:826-831`); this unit **keeps** that posture (D-82 clause 4's discipline, map §3 `B-2`'s Must-NOT): a dropped connection or a non-2xx answer settles the feed's `status()` at a **typed `EngineUnavailable`** and the consumer ships **no backoff, no poll, no queue**. A later `connect()` is the caller's decision, and §2.4 is what makes it safe.

## 7. The live arm — PARKED, its causes, and what would exercise it
