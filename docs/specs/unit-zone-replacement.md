### 7.2 The LAYOUT-EFFECT live assertion (the unit's rendered-layer obligation)

**THE PROPERTY, three limbs, each falsifiable on the assembled app:**

1. **A PANE REARRANGE INSIDE A ZONE MUST NOT RELAYOUT OUTSIDE IT.** Rearranging a tool pane **within** a zone (order change) — or relocating one pane from one zone to another — leaves the **layout, style and paint of the OTHER zones and of the stage unchanged**: the sibling zones' and the stage's measured rects (position and size) are **unchanged**, `#wiki-root`'s mount count stays **exactly 1**, and no stale childless root appears. This is the property the container declaration exists for (`R-1`'s premise), and it is **the one assertion the whole unit's reason-for-existing reduces to**.
2. **ALL ZONES ARE FIXED WITH ANY SCROLLING INTERNAL.** On the assembled surface: each zone container's box holds its position across a page-level scroll attempt and its overflow scrolls **inside** it; a zone's content taller than its box does **not** grow the page.
3. **THE STAGE SCROLLS INTERNALLY.** A document longer than the stage's box scrolls **inside the stage**, and the surrounding chrome (zones, tab strip) does not move.

**HOW IT ENTERS THE BATTERY (the matrix is FULL at 8; `R-10`).** **As a RE-PIN or an EXTENDED row of an existing declared `§5.U` row — NEVER a new slot, and `MATRIX_ROWS` MUST NOT CHANGE.** The carriers, named so no later pass re-derives them: **`U-3` (`uf_panes_12`) — the pane-surface gesture rows, the natural carrier for limb 1; `U-5` (`uf_layout_10`) — the empty-track collapse and the closest carrier for the stage-reclaims-space limb; `U-1`/`U-2`/`U-6`/`U-4` remain the tab/doc-nav/minimize/mount carriers** (`docs/specs/unit-pd-ui-12-slot-host-boundary.md` §8's table, carried). **THE CHOICE OF CARRIER IS A LANDING-TIME RECORD, not this spec's: the landing states which declared row it re-pinned and prints that row's `required` and observed values**, so the assertion is attributable to a declared row rather than to an untracked probe. **A live pass that cannot run reports `PRECONDITION-FAILED` WITH ITS READING ATTACHED (`RCA-11`), never a silent park.** **And the mandatory pre-live leg's status is carried, not fixed here:** `npm run divergence` is **RED at this branch head for an ENVIRONMENTAL reason** (the `/dev/shm` denial class), its harness fix is **its own owed unit**, and **no live battery can honestly be claimed green until that fix lands** — this unit **passes the red precondition through and claims nothing** (`O-5`).
