## 1. What the module is, and what a caller gets

`provident-storeroom` becomes this fork's record engine: **one seam** (`src/main/storeroom.ts`, the ONE `src/**` file that may spell the package, contract §1/§5(f)), **one family** (`src/main/record-engine/display-family.ts`, the `display` family D-23 names), **its delivery**, **the record over an instance the caller built**, and **the renderer client that holds the shape**. The design source is PORTED by hand from the foundation at our pin and never vendored (`ASM-B-1`, D-9).

## 2. The seven declared endpoints — and the counting rule that makes it seven

| # | Endpoint | Signature (the whole of it) | Its refusals, and who answers them |
|---|---|---|---|
| `E1` | `createProfileStore` | `(opts?: ProfileStoreOptions) => StoreInstance`, all members optional (`journalLength`, `constraints`, `listeners`, `filePath`) | Constructs or throws the store's own form; it mints no refusal of its own. **All three config keys are supplied** (contract §3; `RUN`: an absent `journalLength` refuses `ConstructionArgumentMissing` and an absent `listeners` refuses `AddressMalformed`), which is what makes `createProfileStore()` lawful |
| `E2` | `commitLog` | `(record: unknown) => readonly LogEntry[] \| null` | **Total at every argument — it throws nothing.** `null` = *this record was never handed a list*; `[]` = *handed an empty one*. The two are never folded (contract §3) |
| `E3` | `STOREROOM_DECLARED_SURFACE` | `{ members, memberCount, symbolCount }` — the member list **in the instance's own order** | A census, not a derivation: the two counts are LITERALS beside the list, so a release that grows or re-orders a member reds the census instead of passing by length. **`symbolCount` is RE-MEASURED at the installed release and answers `F-3`**: `14` at construction, `15` after one write (contract §3/§5(f)) |
| `E4` | `createDisplayDelivery` | `(sink: (address: readonly string[]) => void) => { entries: readonly DisplayDeliveryEntry[]; subscribe(handler: () => void): () => void }` | The object **declares and never registers**: the one registration per address is the construction's (`cfg.listeners`). | `E6` | `displayConstraintDeclarations` | `(getStore: () => StoreInstance \| null) => readonly DisplayConstraintDeclaration[]` (`{address, id, constraint}`) | The body answers the package's **CLOSED three-arm set** — `true`, `{message, repair}`, `{message, repair?: never}` — with a SENTENCE and **no `reason` token** (see §4). A family that stands and names an entry the parse cannot state is the unrepairable arm |
| `E7` | the client's `subscribe` | `(handler: (reading: DisplayReading \| null) => void) => DisplaySubscription` = `{subscribed: boolean; reason: string \| null; release: () => void}` | **FIVE absent arms, each `subscribed: false` with its own word**: `no-port` · `no-read-route` · `no-changed-route` · `register-threw` · **`no-write-route`** (the SENDER's absence, `B-10` §5: a port that reads and pushes but cannot write leaves the capture with nowhere to land, and folding it into one of the four read-side words would leave a caller unable to tell a wiring fault from a refusal). Never a throw — **and a port member whose GETTER throws is the absent arm too** (`B1-ADV-05(c)`). `release` is total on every arm (contract §3) |

**THE COUNTING RULE FOR `N = 7`, STATED BECAUSE THE SEVEN ARE NOT THE MODULE'S EXPORTS.** The seven are exactly the surface `strata/modules.json` declares for this module, one row each: **`E1`–`E3` the seam's three** (`createProfileStore`, `commitLog`, the declared census) and **`E4`–`E7` the crossing's four** (the delivery factory, the two projections, the client's subscription). **WHAT IS NOT COUNTED, AND WHY**: `createDisplayRecord` and its `read`/`readTab`/`commitTab` verbs, `createDisplayClient` itself, `displayEntryIn`/`displayEntryAdmits`/`displayEntryMemberAdmits`, `displayFamily`/`DISPLAY_FAMILIES`, and `repairDisplay` are the module's **INTERNALS** — a record composed over the caller's instance, a client composed over a realm port, one parse shared by four callers, and a factory whose products are the endpoint `E5`/`E6` publish. **AND THE CLIENT'S `write(tabId, entry)` IS NOT COUNTED IN `N` EITHER** (`B-10` §5): it is a MEMBER of the client `E7` already counts, not a new endpoint — the client is `E7`'s owner and the sender is one more verb on the surface `E7` publishes, exactly as `fill`/`reading`/`displayOf` are. Counting `write` would count the client twice. **A count that counted the internals would count the same crossing twice** (the record is `E2`'s reader and `E4`'s consumer; the client is `E7`'s owner), and the endpoint figure is a lint reading of a declared surface, not a census of every name the barrel exports. The record's own three members are therefore measured by the battery (`entry-domain.test.ts`, `root-invariant.test.ts`) and by nothing in this table.

## 3. The family's payload and its ONE parse, as the layers below read it

- **Address `['display']`, tier `file`, one whole value at the root** — one member per tab id, the key being the **address segment** and never a stored `tabId` (contract §2, `ASM-B1-1`). A leaf lands at `['display', <tabId>]`; a caller routes on a prefix of what the delivery hands it.
- **Payload `DisplayEntry = { documentId: string; caret?: DisplayCaret; view?: DisplayView }`** — D-23's list and no more. `caret` is the fork's caret vocabulary minus its `ragId`; `view` is the scroll position and the visible section.
- **THE MEMBER NAMES ARE AN ALLOWLIST AND `D-23`'s LINE IS MECHANICAL** (`B1-ADV-01`): `documentId`/`caret`/`view` at the entry, `kind`/`anchor`/`focus`/`focused` in a caret, `path`/`offset` in an edge, `offset`/`section` in a view — **and a name outside its own list is REFUSED at every level**, with the member token and a sentence that names document content as the fault. `content`, `text`, `html`, `title`, `markdown`, `body`, `ragId`, `tabId` and a nested document object are refused, never stored and never silently dropped.
- **One parse, one answer record**: `displayEntryIn(held)` answers `{ok: true, entry}` or `{ok: false, reason, message}`, and `displayEntryAdmits(held)` is the same judgement as a predicate. The read, the write route, the constraint **and the client** call it. The admitted arm is the value handed in — **with one stated exception**: an own-`undefined` optional member (`{documentId, caret: undefined}`, the ordinary optional spread) is the LAWFUL ABSENCE and is not carried into the admitted entry, because the crossing refuses `undefined` (`B1-ADV-16`).
- **TWO `reason` tokens, in TWO domains** — `malformed-display` (a payload's shape: no plain record, a tab id that is no non-empty string, **or a tab id that is a prototype key**) and `malformed-display-entry` (a member's fault: an undeclared name, an out-of-domain value, a path with a hole, a value outside the crossing's boundary). **THERE IS NO THIRD TOKEN, AND THE DEAD EXPORT IS GONE**: the fix round's ruling is that the constraint answers the package's closed set with a SENTENCE and no token (§4), so `DISPLAY_UNUSABLE_REASON` — exported and read by nothing — is DELETED from `display-shape.ts` and from the barrel. The record's write route answers the parse's own `reason` and sentence and adds **the address it refused — the LEAF**, never the root.

- **THE CROSSING'S BOUNDARY IS PART OF THE PARSE** (`B1-ADV-02`): the whole entry is walked by the foundation's `isBoundary` (`../Provident-Electron/src/main/view-record/index.ts:527-533`), ported and extended — a cycle, a non-plain record (`Map`/`Set`/`Date`), a lone surrogate and an array with a hole are refused. So a value the store would throw on answers this module's record instead, before any store verb. `path` is walked BY INDEX for the same reason: `.every` skips a hole.
- **The placeholder is `{}`** — one plain record at the root, stating no tab, written by the creation's own repair so a nested write has a parent to land under.

## 4. The constraint, the repair, and the reach of `D-105`

- **THE CONSTRAINT BODY ANSWERS THE PACKAGE'S CLOSED SET, AND THE THREE INSTANTS ARE THREE DIFFERENT RECORDS** (`RUN`, measured at the installed release by counting the record's own members as each arrives — and **it is the one place a hand-port goes wrong**):

| Instant | The record handed over | Members | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| the CONSTRAINT BODY, at any instant | `{address, tier, value, attempt, op}` | **5** — **no `write`** | `true` / `{message, repair}` / `{message, repair?: never}` |
| a REGISTRATION repair | `{address, id, tier, value, op, pending, write}` | **7** | `write(address, tier, value)` — THREE positions; a fourth throws `TypeError` before any store act |
| a LANDING repair | `{address, id, op, tier, value, pending}` | **6** — **no `write`** | `store.write(address, tier, value, <the context's own commit>)` |

  **NO INSTANT IS "THE OTHER ONE PLUS A MEMBER"** and this table replaces every earlier statement of that shape. The repair branches on the record's own `write` member, never on the instant's name, and credits `{ok: true, op}` with **that write's own op**; a body answering non-`true` with a repair that credits no op refuses the construction at rank `19`, and an op that landed while the re-run still refuses is rank `20` (contract §2).
- **`D-105`'S ARM, AND WHAT EACH NODE ACTUALLY JUDGES** (`B1-ADV-03`): the body sees `context.value` — **the value the act LANDED, at the node the act's address names** — so a root write is judged as a whole family, a tab write as one whole entry (allowlist, every member's domain, the boundary), and a member write as one name and one value. **It never sees a sibling.** The reach of the arm is therefore exactly this: *no write THIS MODULE routes can leave the store holding a member the read cannot state*, because the tab node and the member node each refuse one, and `commitTab`'s parse refuses it before the store is touched. **A member that arrived from an outside writer** (an older image, a second process, a store built without the pair) has no write instant to answer it, and the arm that answers it is the **READ**'s: `read()` answers `null` — the DECLARED ABSENCE — for a family it cannot state whole, and `readTab` answers `null` for the one node it cannot state, while a statable sibling still answers. **A read may not repair and must not drop, so this is the only arm left; the cost is stated rather than hidden** (an unstatable profile reads as no saved state until a statable ROOT write replaces it, which `root-invariant.test.ts` measures).
- **The one constraint is `display-must-stand`**, one declaration at `['display']`, and its arms are the contract's §2: absence at the root is repaired with the placeholder; a standing family naming an unstatable entry is REFUSED with **no repair** at a write instant; a write at or below a tab id is judged at its own node and **never repaired**.

## 5. The delivery, the record and the client

- **The entry is `{address, handle, listener}` — three own members with the THIRD REQUIRED**; it **carries no value and never a reading**. `handle` is minted by a module counter (`record-engine:<ordinal>@<address>`) so no handle can equal a constraint `id`.
- **The body's guarantees** (contract §3, `D-26`'s items 1–2): it **writes nothing**, it **contains its own throw**, and inside it **the fan-out runs first and the sink after**, on every delivery. The sink is handed the **triggering address and exactly one argument**; a **subscriber is handed nothing at all and is called at arity 0**.
- **A NESTED DELIVERY IS COALESCED, NEVER RECURSED** (`B1-ADV-08`, measured: a subscriber that wrote re-entered the fan-out once per write; 380 levels produced a `RangeError` inside the fan-out, truncated the delivery, and left the outer write answering `{ok: true}` because the body contains its own throw). While a fan-out is in flight a further delivery sets a pending flag, and the outer fan-out runs **ONE more pass after its sink** — two passes for any amount of re-entrancy, and the count is what `family-and-fanout.test.ts` measures.
- **The record**: `read()` (the family whole or `null` — a read writes nothing), `readTab(tabId)`, `commitTab(tabId, entry)` (shape arm → the parse → **then** one leaf write). **A tab id that is a prototype key (`__proto__`, `constructor`, `prototype`) is REFUSED by the shape arm** (`B1-ADV-05(a)`: it committed, vanished from `read()` and became the reading's PROTOTYPE).
- **The client's port is structural and LOCAL**: `{ read(): Promise<DisplayReading | null> | DisplayReading | null; onChanged(handler: () => void): unknown; write?(tabId: string, entry: DisplayEntry): unknown }`, every member optional because each absence is one of the five arms. **`write` IS THE SENDER** (`B-10` §5): POSITIONAL, because the address and the whole entry are two facts about one act; the realm's transport answers the record's own `DisplayWriteAnswer` (or a promise of one) and **the client judges nothing — it passes the answer through untouched**; a write NEVER touches the held shape (the store's delivery → the push → the listener's re-read is the ONE route by which the held shape moves), the entry handed out is a bounded structural COPY, and the record's own answer is `null` when the transport throws or rejects. **THREE HOLDING RULES, ALL MEASURED AS FAULTS FIRST** (`B1-ADV-04`/`05(b)`): **a failure is not an absence** — no route, a throw, a rejection or a value that is no plain record leaves the standing shape INTACT; **the latest-STARTED fill wins** — a monotonic sequence guard, not the order the answers arrive; and **`displayOf` reads OWN members only and hands an entry out only through the family's own parse**, so an absent tab answers `null` and never `Object.prototype`.
- **ONE DISCLOSED DIVERGENCE FROM THE CONTRACT'S §3** (`F-R4`): the client takes the parse **by VALUE** (`displayEntryIn`), where §3 says the renderer half takes the payload *"by `import type`"*. A second spelling of "an entry" in the renderer would be a second authority for the family's shape (`D-43`), and `display-shape.js` is `import`-free, so nothing main-side travels with it. The proper carrier is a `src/shared/` module, which is outside this unit's write scope and is filed for `B-2` (`ASM-B1-3`).
